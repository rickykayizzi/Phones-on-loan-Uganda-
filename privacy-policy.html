Who We Are

Quick Phones Uganda ("Quick Phones," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website quickphones.online. We help customers in Uganda compare smartphones and phone financing options, including devices offered through independent providers such as Watu, TakeNow and MOGO.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at rickykats14@gmail.com or +256 768 372 800.

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few limited ways:

  • Information you give us directly — such as your name, phone number, or message content when you contact us through WhatsApp, email, or a contact form on this site.
  • Automatically collected information — such as your approximate location (country/city), browser type, device type, pages visited, and how you arrived at our site. This is collected through Google Analytics (see below).
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small files used by analytics and, if enabled, advertising services to understand site usage and display relevant content.

We do not require account creation to browse this website, and we do not collect payment card details — all phone loan applications and payments are handled directly by the relevant financing provider (e.g. Watu, TakeNow, MOGO), not by Quick Phones.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries about phones, prices, or phone loan options
  • To understand how visitors use our site, so we can improve content and navigation
  • To communicate with you if you reach out via WhatsApp, email, or our contact form
  • To detect and prevent misuse of our website

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use quickphones.online — for example, which pages are popular and how people find our site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymized/aggregated usage data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Third-Party Links and Services

Our site contains links to third-party services, including WhatsApp (for messaging) and websites belonging to independent phone financing providers such as Watu, TakeNow and MOGO. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to review their own privacy policies before sharing personal information with them.

If we display advertising on this site (for example, through Google AdSense), those advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's Partner Sites policy, and manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.

Children's Privacy

Quick Phones Uganda is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Data Retention & Security

We retain messages and enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and keep basic records, and we take reasonable steps to protect information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • You can choose not to provide personal information, though this may limit our ability to respond to your enquiry.
  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
