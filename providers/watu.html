How To Pay WATU Uganda (USSD & App Payment Guide)

One of the most common questions customers ask is:

"How do I pay my WATU phone loan?"

WATU payments can be made through the WATU application or through USSD payment options.

Payment Using USSD

For supported networks:

Dial: *185*5*15#

Follow the instructions and enter your reference number.

Your reference number is your WATU registration number.

If Your Money Is On MTN Mobile Money

Dial: *217*777#

Complete the payment instructions displayed on your phone.

Keep your registration number safe. It is your key when making repayments.
