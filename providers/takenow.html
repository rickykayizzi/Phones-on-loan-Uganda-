What Is TakeNow Uganda?

TakeNow is a smartphone financing program that allows customers to get selected phones by paying a deposit and completing the remaining payments through the available repayment system.

In Uganda, TakeNow mainly focuses on:

  • TECNO smartphones
  • Infinix smartphones
  • itel smartphones

TakeNow does not cover every phone brand. Customers looking for other brands can explore different financing options depending on availability.

How TakeNow Works

The process is designed to be simple:

Step 1: Choose an eligible TECNO, Infinix or itel smartphone.

Step 2: Provide the required identification.

Step 3: Pay the required deposit.

Step 4: Receive your phone and follow the agreed repayment plan.

The cheapest mistake is asking questions before signing. The expensive mistake is asking after.

TakeNow Uganda Requirements

Many people searching for TakeNow want to know what they need before visiting a shop.

Generally, customers should prepare:

  • Original National ID
  • Required deposit depending on the selected phone
  • Meeting TakeNow eligibility requirements

Requirements and terms can change, so always confirm the latest information before completing your purchase.

How To Pay TakeNow Uganda (Mpola Mpola Payment Guide)

One of the most searched questions about TakeNow is:

"How do I pay TakeNow?"

TakeNow repayments are made through the available payment channels, including MTN Mobile Money Mpola Mpola options where applicable.

Payment Steps

1. Dial *165*5# on your MTN line.

2. Select the TakeNow/Mpola Mpola option.

3. Follow the instructions displayed on your phone.

4. Confirm payment using your Mobile Money PIN.

Always keep your payment confirmation messages and contact your provider if you have any payment questions.
