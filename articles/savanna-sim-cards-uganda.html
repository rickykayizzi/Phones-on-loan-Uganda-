What is a Savanna SIM Card?

Savanna is a telecommunications network in Uganda offering voice services, prepaid SIM cards and the Funna Unlimited Internet & Voice packages. Its services are designed for customers who want affordable internet access for streaming football, watching videos, browsing social media, online learning and everyday communication.

If your phone supports VoLTE (Voice over LTE), you'll enjoy the best calling experience and reliable internet performance on the Savanna network.
