What is the MTN Kabode Pro?

The MTN Kabode Pro is the name used by MTN Uganda for the ZTE Blade A36 smartphone. It is designed for customers looking for an affordable Android smartphone with flexible payment options instead of paying the full cash amount at once.

If you're upgrading from a feature phone or an older Android device, the Kabode Pro offers a larger display, Android 15, a long-lasting battery and enough performance for daily use.

How MTN Phones on Loan Work in Uganda

The MTN Phones on Loan program is designed to make smartphones more affordable for Ugandans who cannot pay the full amount upfront. Instead of saving for months before buying a phone, customers can pay a smaller deposit and continue paying the remaining balance over an agreed repayment period.

For 2026, one of the most popular devices is the MTN Kabode Pro, which is based on the ZTE Blade A36. It combines Android 15, a large 90Hz display and a 5,000mAh battery, making it suitable for students, small business owners, social media users and anyone upgrading from a feature phone.

The biggest advantage of MTN phone financing is that it gives customers access to a modern smartphone without needing to pay the full cash price immediately.

Who Can Apply for MTN Phones on Loan?

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements set by MTN and its financing partners. Requirements may change over time, but customers are generally expected to provide identification and complete verification before approval.

  • Valid National ID
  • Registered MTN SIM Card
  • Required deposit payment
  • Successful verification
  • Acceptance of repayment terms

Approval depends on meeting the current requirements and completing the application process successfully.

How to Apply for the MTN Kabode Pro

  1. Visit an authorised MTN outlet or participating dealer.
  2. Choose the MTN Kabode Pro smartphone.
  3. Present your National ID.
  4. Complete the customer verification process.
  5. Pay the required UGX 100,000 deposit.
  6. Receive approval and collect your phone.
  7. Continue paying the remaining balance according to your repayment schedule.

Before applying, ask the dealer about the latest repayment plan and any promotional offers that may be available.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the MTN Kabode Pro?

The MTN Kabode Pro is MTN Uganda's affordable smartphone based on the ZTE Blade A36. It runs Android 15, features a 6.75-inch 90Hz display, 4GB RAM, 64GB storage and a 5,000mAh battery, making it ideal for everyday use.

How much is the MTN Kabode Pro deposit in 2026?

The current deposit is UGX 100,000. Previously, customers could get the phone with an UGX 80,000 deposit, but the amount increased due to price adjustments.

Can I get an MTN phone on loan without a deposit?

No. Customers are normally required to pay the required deposit before collecting the phone. The deposit helps reduce the remaining loan balance.

Which Android version does the MTN Kabode Pro use?

The phone comes with Android 15 out of the box, giving users access to the latest Android features and security improvements.

Is the MTN Kabode Pro good for gaming?

The phone handles light games and everyday apps well. However, demanding games with high graphics settings may not run as smoothly because it is an entry-level smartphone.

Can I expand the storage?

Yes. The MTN Kabode Pro includes a dedicated microSD card slot, allowing you to add more storage for photos, videos and applications.
